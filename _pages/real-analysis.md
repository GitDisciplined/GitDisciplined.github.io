# Real Analysis

Real Analysis is a branch of mathematical analysis dealing with real numbers, sequences, and functions.

## Limits and Continuity

The formal definition of a limit is:

$$ \lim_{{x \to a}} f(x) = L $$

Where \( L \) is the limit value of \( f(x) \) as \( x \) approaches \( a \).

## Sequences and Series

Sequences are ordered lists of numbers. A series is the sum of a sequence’s terms.

You can explore deeper topics like convergence, divergence, and Cauchy sequences.
