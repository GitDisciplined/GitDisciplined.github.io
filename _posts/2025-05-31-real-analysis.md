## Limits and Continuity

In this post, we'll explore the concept of limits.

The definition of a limit is:

$$
\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L
$$

where for every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists $\delta > 0$ such that if $|x - a| < \delta$, then $|f(x) - L| < \varepsilon$.
